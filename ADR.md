# Architecture Decision Records

## ADR-001: Why Earl Grey is the default tea
**Context**: We needed a default tea for our un-brewable menu.
**Decision**: Earl Grey was chosen due to its high enterprise synergy and command presence (Captain Picard approved).
**Consequences**: The teapot remains empty, but with a more distinguished aroma profile.

## ADR-002: Why we chose 4180 as the port
**Context**: Which port should a teapot use?
**Decision**: Port 4180 was selected. 418 is the HTTP status code, and 0 implies our baseline inventory level.
**Consequences**: Port 4180 is now bound permanently to refusal.

## ADR-003: Why coffee support was rejected
**Context**: Requesters occasionally ask for coffee.
**Decision**: We are a teapot. We respond with prejudice (HTTP 418) to coffee requests.
**Consequences**: We might offend coffee drinkers, but RFC compliance is critical to enterprise stability.
